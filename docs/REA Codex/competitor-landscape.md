# Competitive landscape and public product teardown

As of **21 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)**. Prepared for the Consumer Insight Portal REA investigation. Source register: [competitor-sources.json](competitor-sources.json).

## Findings that change the strategy

1. The named competitor is **Circana Unify+**. Its product page advertises collaboration, custom reports, alerts, performance narratives, conversational and agentic AI. Its **15 September 2026** Analysis Builder announcement adds presentation-ready charts, written summaries and PowerPoint/Excel/PDF export. Exported chart editability has not been established. [Product](https://www.circana.com/solutions/unify-plus), [announcement](https://www.circana.com/post/circana-enhances-unify-with-ai-powered-analysis-builder-for-faster-more-intuitive-reporting).
2. The closest survey and audience comparisons include **GWI, YouGov Profiles, Statista Consumer Insights, Morning Consult, MRI-Simmons**. Their recurring pattern is a workflow: define an audience, inspect comparisons, preserve the result, share the story, and move it into another business tool. Portal exploration is real; the next opportunity is completing this workflow. [GWI](https://www.gwi.com/platform), [YouGov](https://yougov.com/business/products/profiles), [Statista](https://www.statista.com/consumer-insights/).
3. **AI is already widely advertised**. A generic chatbot would offer little defensible differentiation. Grounded answers must expose question wording, wave, filters, base, method and exportable evidence. Statista's question-level charts and separate survey modules are useful reference designs. [Charts](https://www.statista.com/getting-started/consumer-insights-charts), [modules](https://www.statista.com/getting-started/consumer-insights-survey-modules).
4. **Transactions, purchase panels, location observations and social conversations are different products and different evidence.** Circana, NIQ, Numerator, Placer.ai and Brandwatch cannot be matched by adding a chart to self-reported survey data. Competing in these domains requires licensed sources, proper methodology and commercial validation.
5. Local REA workstream findings establish genuine React exploration, crosstabs, trends, compare and search; CSV/PNG/print exports and legacy XLSX. They did not establish persistent React dashboards, collaboration or AI routes. This is the baseline for opportunity statements below. Production operation, current row counts and live inventory are not inferred from repository documentation.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a **bounded set of 23 vendors/product families**, selected across direct syndicated intelligence, brand trackers, retail behavior, survey execution and adjacent analysis. It is not an assertion that every global competitor has been identified. A supplier may also be a competitor or an integration partner. Product families and corporate owners must not be counted as necessarily independent vendors; ownership consolidation was not separately audited.

Competitor examination is a **public functional teardown**: official product pages, documentation and dated announcements establish advertised inputs, analysis functions and outputs. We did not inspect paid competitor tenants, proprietary APIs, source code, internal storage, models or contracts. Consequently, private architecture, actual accuracy, latency, uptime and customer satisfaction remain unknown. No claim of independent vendor certification is made.

**Evidence labels:** advertised = stated by an official vendor page or its official search-index excerpt; observed local = established by the primary portal REA investigation; inferred opportunity = analyst recommendation, not a proven defect or committed implementation. Sources carry access date 2026-10-21 in the JSON register. Marketing counts are omitted from the comparison because panel access, annual interviews, profile variables and actual per-question bases are incompatible quantities.

**Retrieval depth:** follow-up retrieval obtained substantive full official product/help-page bodies for **22 of23 primary vendor sources**, using web.open, agent-reach Jina Reader, or the primary REA workstream. The Mintel product page remained unavailable (timeout/maintenance503); its official **9 January2024 launch announcement** was opened and corroborates research/analyst grounding. All supplemental sources now have body content except Numerator help article **S03**, which returned401 and remains official-search-excerpt evidence. Per-source attempts, hashes and limitations are preserved in [competitor-retrieval.json](evidence/competitor-retrieval.json).

**Circana retrieval:** the Unify+ product page was captured by REA ([artifact](evidence/circana-public-page.json)). Initial web.open attempts did not yield the full Analysis Builder article, but agent-reach Jina Reader subsequently recovered its body. Its date, presentation-ready charts, written summaries and PowerPoint/Excel/PDF exports were confirmed in that body. Exported chart editability remains unverified.

**Publication-date audit:** no explicitly dated feature announcement used here falls after 21 October 2026. Only established publisher dates were assessed; undated pages remain undated. No publication date was manufactured from relative search/crawl labels.

Morning Consult initially redirected to an inaccessible host in web.open; Jina Reader subsequently retrieved its substantive official product-page body. NIQ, Numerator, MRI-Simmons, Displayr, Forsta and Crunch bodies were also directly opened; Statista and Euromonitor bodies were recovered through Jina after web403 responses. These retrieval upgrades substantiate public functionality statements, not private production behavior. Search-index freshness and crawl dates are not product launch dates. Circana's dated September announcement precedes the assessment date. Navigation references to 2027 material on public pages do not establish future feature availability.

## Prioritized response to the market

### P0 — Make every existing result defensible

Close access-control findings and establish consistent query, base, privacy and provenance contracts across UI, endpoints and exports. A competitor feature checklist cannot compensate for a result that cannot be reproduced. Preserve the explicit unweighted policy until the research owner approves a methodological change. Repository wave counts are inconsistent across documents; replace hard-coded claims with a generated, dated data manifest.

Acceptance: users can inspect wording, fieldwork dates, selected wave(s), numerator/denominator, unweighted base, suppression reason and method for every supported result; comparable views produce matching results; unauthorized dataset access fails at each serving layer.

### P1 — Turn analysis into a reusable weekly workflow

Persist audiences, boards and analysis definitions; attach notes and provenance; support authorized team sharing; ship one reusable weekly insight deck before many output formats. Add carefully defined change alerts after comparable-wave and low-base rules work.

Acceptance: saving/reopening a workspace reproduces results for a pinned data version; new waves can refresh a template without changing its definitions; exports carry complete analysis context; alert explanations name the actual trigger and sample caveats.

### P2 — Add a constrained research assistant and integrations

Build natural-language access to the same tested aggregate functions. Retrieval can add approved analyst commentary, but numeric answers must come from deterministic queries. Make follow-up questions useful: “Which income cohorts account for this difference?” “Show the comparable prior wave.” “Create a sourced retail strategy brief.” Ship read-only external aggregate API access before general agent integration.

Acceptance: authorized scope, numeric agreement, citation completeness, unsupported-question abstention, prompt-injection resistance and suppression consistency are measured with a review dataset. Marketing copy must not promise hallucination-free output.

### P3 — Expand evidence only where buyers will pay

Pilot a narrow purchase/retail-data partnership, then assess value. Follow-up custom studies can start as a service/partner workflow. International expansion, campaign attribution, media activation and social/location evidence each require a separate business case and data contract.

Acceptance: commercial rights, cost to serve, supported population, refresh SLA, privacy obligations and measurable customer decision value established before broad rollout. Additional sources remain visibly distinct and are not used to fabricate causal or respondent-level relationships.

## Proposed advantage to test, not a proven superiority claim

A focused proposition is **“the fastest defensible weekly view of US consumer change, tied to practical retail decisions.”** The prospective advantage is rapid weekly trend investigation plus Coresight analyst context and reproducible evidence. This is a strategic hypothesis; no head-to-head customer experiment establishes superiority.

Run a benchmark with target retail/brand analysts using matched research tasks: detect a meaningful change, locate its audience drivers, produce a review-ready brief and reproduce the source. Measure time to a correct sourced answer, material error rate, analyst confidence, export rework and repeat weekly usage. Compare the licensed products customers already use, with comparable data availability. Do not claim to outperform Circana on transaction measurement or GWI on global coverage without the corresponding data.
