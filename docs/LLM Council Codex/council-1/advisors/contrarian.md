# Contrarian — Council 1

The largest strategic risk is treating a sound remediation program as proof of a winning product. Rights enforcement, correct bases and reproducibility are obligations; customers may never pay a premium for them. The proposed weekly US retail workflow remains an untested positioning hypothesis. No paying persona, lost-deal evidence, renewal driver or budget establishes that saved boards are the next constraint.

Keep G01–G03 as restricted-release gates, but verify the actual deployed identity and release before redesigning around a local mismatch. Separate correctness defects requiring closure from optional platform redesign. A universal analytical contract is useful only if implemented incrementally through one working journey; rebuilding every interface first could consume the entire investment.

Audience sophistication can reduce utility: the latest displayed wave has n=400 before question routing or behavioral cuts. More intersections may produce suppressed or unstable cells, not better answers. Validate feasible audiences and comparable question coverage before promising a builder or driver analysis. Alerts over rotating questions may amplify noise despite FDR; AI could make that noise more persuasive.

Do not benchmark all 23 families as substitutable competitors. Circana’s purchase evidence and GWI’s audience breadth are different buying reasons. Test the customer’s existing spreadsheet, analyst service and incumbent subscription as alternatives. Partner for licensed behavioral data only when it resolves a specific paid decision; reject broad parity and generic AI investment for now.

**First move:** run a supervised, consented weekly-brief pilot using existing capabilities and manual analyst support, alongside immediate release-risk verification. Record unanswered decisions, usable bases, correction work, repeat use and willingness to pay. Fund one export/workspace slice only if workflow friction, rather than missing evidence, is the demonstrated bottleneck.
