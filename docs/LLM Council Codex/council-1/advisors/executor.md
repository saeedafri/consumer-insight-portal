# Executor — Council1 independent recommendation

Choose trust-gated delivery of one recurring customer task. The preliminary proposal is directionally sound but six bands risk becoming an unfunded platform rewrite. Staffing, buyer priorities and paid demand are unknown; neither AI nor a general-purpose workspace should become a commitment yet.

**First move:** have the release owner reproduce one authorized analyst journey on a recorded frontend/backend/data release, including Trends and Compare. Establish whether G03 is deployment drift or a product defect before estimating fixes. In parallel, product can observe buyers creating their weekly brief without expanding external data access.

Priority one is the restricted-release boundary: resolve G01–G03 with an identity/API owner and prove allowed/denied behavior across catalog, search, API, static files and caches. Research and data owners then validate the particular metrics needed for that brief: overlapping comparisons, bases, disclosure, provenance, exports and publication/reconciliation failures. Do not make a wholesale result-schema migration a prerequisite when a compatible adapter can establish consistent outputs.

The first commercial slice should reopen a saved analysis against a pinned release and produce one customer-selected report format. Reuse frozen publications and existing cohort infrastructure only after testing their version and membership limitations. Acceptance: a buyer recreates the same figures, sources and bases after publication of the next wave; exports match the screen; fewer corrections and shorter completion time than their current workflow. Set the success threshold with the buyer before the pilot.

Build the research-specific contract and persistence; evaluate a reporting library before building a deck engine. Defer alerts, broad behavioral segmentation, external APIs and AI until repeat use establishes demand. Partner for purchase data only after rights and unit economics pass review. Circana’s data assets cannot be reproduced through interface work.
