# First Principles Thinker

A buyer pays to reduce uncertainty in a consequential decision. Neither 371 waves nor feature parity establishes that CIP reduces it. The evidence supports historical survey exploration; the proposed “weekly US retail decision” positioning remains unvalidated. Identify which recurring decision, decision owner and alternative workflow create willingness to pay before committing to the proposed roadmap.

Prioritize A, with a narrower interpretation: access protection and correct supported answers are release conditions, while commercial discovery proceeds concurrently. G01–G03 and G04–G10 matter because unauthorized, irreproducible or misbased evidence destroys the deliverable's value. Do not make every statistical enhancement a prerequisite for every workflow; explicitly constrain claims and disable unsupported comparisons. Unweighted data can support a disclosed descriptive use case without pretending to measure national prevalence.

The first differentiated product should complete one recurring job: a saved question becomes a correctly sourced, reproducible weekly brief that a buyer can reuse. Build the analysis specification, evidence contract and frozen/live distinction; reuse existing publications and cohorts. Buy commodity rendering or partner for analysis tooling where licence economics beat maintenance. Defer the general audience builder, alerts and AI until observed friction makes each necessary. At n=400 in the latest displayed wave, many fine behavioral segments may be analytically unusable; richer filters cannot manufacture evidence.

Circana/NIQ transaction coverage cannot be recreated through interface work. Partner only when a paying decision requires purchase facts. A competitor's advertised chatbot is no reason to build one.

**First move:** with a research owner and paying or qualified prospective buyer, replay one recently delivered decision brief end to end. Record required evidence, missing evidence, manual effort and the competing substitute. Choose the smallest protected, methodologically valid workflow that demonstrably reduces correction time and earns repeat use.
