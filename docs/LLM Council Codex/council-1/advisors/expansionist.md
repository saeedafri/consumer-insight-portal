# Expansionist — Council 1

The preliminary plan undervalues CIP’s reusable research assets and over-sequences discovery behind engineering gates. Mandatory access and research corrections should gate customer exposure; they need not postpone buyer interviews, report prototypes or partner qualification.

The strongest upside is a repeatable **retail decision brief**: what changed, which supported audience differs, what Coresight’s approved research adds, and which question remains unanswered. Harmonised history, own-question bases, existing cohorts and frozen publications can support this loop. These are foundations to extend, not proof of a defensible moat. Coresight commentary availability, reuse rights and buyer demand still need validation.

Prioritize one saved audience plus a reproducible brief over a general workspace platform. GWI, MRI-Simmons and Circana already advertise broad workflow features; generic boards offer weak differentiation. Build reusable metric definitions and provenance internally. Buy commodity rendering where appropriate, provided exports preserve exact bases and citations. Test a tightly scoped cited assistant during prototyping rather than assuming it belongs several stages later; production still requires validated calculations and rights enforcement.

Elevate follow-up research from an optional appendix into a commercial experiment. A request-to-brief service through existing fielding partners could address questions absent from the tracker and reveal which new variables buyers value. Do not assume response capacity or promise fielding times. Qualify one aggregate purchase-context partner in parallel; avoid committing to transaction licences before paid demand and margin evidence.

Tradeoff: this narrower offer delays broad collaboration and international parity, but tests a monetizable decision loop sooner.

**First move:** ask representative retail buyers to bring a recent decision and recreate its evidence brief using approved existing outputs. Measure preparation time, corrections, uncovered questions and willingness to pay for recurrence; use findings to select the first vertical slice.
