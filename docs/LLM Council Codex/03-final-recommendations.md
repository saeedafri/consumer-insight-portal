# Final changes recommended for the Consumer Insight Portal

Date: 21 October2026, Asia/Kolkata. Status: recommendations validated through two Council passes, not implemented changes or a superiority certification. [Final verdict](council-2/06-chairman-verdict.md) contains agreements, disagreement and remaining uncertainty.

## Investment decision

Preserve the React/FastAPI/serving architecture and staff research assets. Close restricted-release blockers, validate one consequential buyer decision, then deliver one reusable evidence brief. Avoid a general platform rewrite or a feature-parity roadmap across unrelated competitor categories.

The differentiator to test is **useful retail evidence that changes or accelerates a real decision, with less total work and reproducible support**. Traceability alone is a quality requirement; novel/useful evidence or demonstrable total-cost savings must establish incremental value.

## Pilot decision contract

Capture the economic buyer, actual user, recipients, business decision/action options, deadline, required recurrence, geography/population, decision-relevant effect, evidence tolerance, existing sources/subscriptions and current expenditure. Distinguish a new budget request from an existing enterprise licence already available to the user.

Map every required fact to question/wording, wave/date, routing, answering base, audience incidence, source quality and rights. Use four outcomes: **supported, insufficient, incompatible, missing**. Map a missing fact to questionnaire/research or partner evidence, not a fabricated estimate.

Begin with a recent retrospective replay to discover the workflow. Before investment conclusions, run prospective repeats across a small varied prospect set including refusals/nonconverters. A5–8-person discovery sample can expose friction; it cannot estimate market-wide demand or establish superiority.

## Pilot scorecard and stop authority

Numerical thresholds, capacity ceiling and review date must be agreed with the responsible owners before pilot spending; none is invented here.

| Dimension | Record | Stop or narrow when |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical validity | Required evidence, recruitment/coverage, usable base, sensitivity, external benchmark where appropriate | Decision cannot be supported; restrict claim/population or change research design |
| Rights/access | Source/aggregate/AI/export/recipient/retention matrix and route parity | Required use lacks rights or protected content is exposed |
| Decision impact | What changed/confirmed/accelerated and what action followed | No incremental decision value over current bundle |
| Total work | Buyer effort, analyst preparation, rework, support, routine/exception weeks | Costs or manual intervention exceed agreed ceiling |
| Reproducibility | Frozen replay, comparable refresh, complete citations and export fidelity | Output cannot be reproduced or unsupported refresh changes meaning |
| Commercial commitment | Budget owner, procurement step/payment, replacement/complement and displacement | Praise/reuse fails to convert to an agreed purchasing signal |
| Self-service value | Tasks completed unaided, staff interventions and repeat behavior | Concierge demand is misread as software demand |
| Unit economics | Realizable price less fieldwork/licences/rendering/support/maintenance/opportunity cost | Sustainable margin is not plausible at agreed recurrence |

Research can veto unsupported claims. Security/rights owners can veto exposure. Commercial/product owners stop expansion at the agreed review; stopping does not cancel lawful customer obligations. Deliver contracted work safely and preserve the decision record. Sunk engineering cost does not justify continuation.

## Build buy partner and defer

**Build:** research-specific metric/result specification, harmonisation/comparability rules, source/base provenance, resource-policy integration, saved-analysis semantics and reproducibility. Extend existing code incrementally through the selected slice.

**Evaluate buying:** document rendering, identity infrastructure and generic reporting components. Compare integration, licences, control of hidden export data, deployment footprint and maintenance; no vendor price or contract has been assumed.

**Partner:** licensed purchase/POS/receipt context or follow-up sample/fielding when a specific funded question requires it. Preserve distinct sources, populations, denominators and permitted redistribution.

**Defer:** broad dashboard designers, arbitrary fine segmentation, global coverage, mass alerts, general customer APIs/MCP, generic chat, synthetic respondents, forecasting and causal pricing/promotion. Each is a separate decision, not permanently rejected. A cited descriptive assistant is eligible only after typed deterministic metrics, rights, abstention and held-out evaluation exist.

## Changes made because of the Councils

The preliminary plan proposed six broad stages. Council1 reduced the first investment to one saved brief and one format; moved buyer learning alongside release work; added data-adequacy and complete-cost gates; and challenged three simultaneous expansion experiments.

Council2 added a decision contract before workspace funding; prospective and varied-prospect validation; explicit population/selection-bias boundaries; independent evidence checks where available; recipient/AI/export rights; current-subscription and internal-service counterfactuals; self-service versus concierge measurement; exception-week/cannibalization economics; and named stop authority.

All31 gaps remain in the [register](../REA%20Codex/02-gap-register.md). The Councils changed investment sequence, not their observed status. Technical closure is specified in [implementation specifications](../REA%20Codex/05-implementation-specifications.md). No source fix, deployment, purchase, supplier contact or customer message was performed.
